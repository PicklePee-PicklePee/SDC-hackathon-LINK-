# SDC-hackathon-LINK- AI Training Platform for Teams

Link turns your company's SOPs into employee training. Owners manage content, and interns learn through modules, quizzes, and an AI assistant that answers from your documents and cites its sources.

Training domains: **Culinary SOPs**, **Tech onboarding**, and **Customer support**.

## For owners

1. **Sign up** and create a workspace. You become its owner.
2. **Add content.** Load the optional sample SOPs (10 per domain) or add your own documents.
3. **Invite interns.** Share your workspace join code.
4. **Approve requests.** Open the owner portal and approve interns who ask to join. They can't see anything until you approve them.
5. **Set up the AI assistant.** Choose its persona, then run indexing so it can answer from your documents. Indexing uses the GPU and asks you to confirm first.

## For interns

1. **Sign up** and enter the join code from your owner.
2. **Wait for approval.** You get access once the owner approves you.
3. **Learn.** Work through training modules, quizzes, and sandboxes.
4. **Ask the AI assistant.** It answers only from your company's documents and shows its sources.

## Notes

- Sample SOPs are examples, not verified company policy.
- If the AI worker isn't connected, AI features show as unavailable.
